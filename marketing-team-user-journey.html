Zero State

  • Role selection determines which dashboard template loads after onboarding

Zero State — No Breaches

  • SLA filter shows "✓ All leads within SLA — great work!"
  • Green banner instead of red
  • SLA health bars all green
  • No breach cards visible

Edge Cases

  • SDR on leave/OOO → system auto-reassigns before SLA breach, not after
  • Lead in non-business-hours timezone → SLA clock pauses and resumes at next business hour
  • SDR logged a call but Salesforce task not created → grace period of 15 min before alert
  • SDR SLA <50% for 3 days → escalation email to their manager automatically
  • Marketer manually reassigns → SDR receives Slack ping immediately

Why This Matters for Marketing

  • Marketing spends budget to acquire these leads — an SDR going silent wastes that spend
  • Leads go cold fast: 78% of buyers choose the vendor that responds first (research-backed)
  • Marketing visibility into SDR performance closes the handoff gap
  • This screen is only visible to marketing managers + RevOps — not junior SDRs
