1
Neon One — Marketing
Campaign Launch
  • Neon One runs campaigns targeting nonprofits looking for a CRM
  • Channels: Google Ads, LinkedIn, Meta, email sequences, webinars, SEO content
  • Goal: drive nonprofits to neonone.com to request a demo
2
Full Circle — Digital Source Tracker
Anonymous Visitor Lands on Website
  • A nonprofit staffer clicks a LinkedIn ad and arrives on neonone.com
  • Full Circle's first-party tracking pixel fires and captures UTM parameters (which ad, which campaign, which channel)
  • Every page they visit is recorded — /pricing, /nonprofits, /demo — even though they haven't filled any form yet
  • Session is stored anonymously and will be matched to their identity later
3
Website Form
Prospect Submits "Request a Demo" Form
  • Visitor fills out the demo request form: name, work email, org name, current CRM, size
  • They are now a known identity — Full Circle retroactively links all their anonymous sessions to this email
  • Full campaign journey is now complete: first ad click → pages visited → form fill
4
Full Circle — Campaign Attribution
Attribution Recorded in Salesforce
  • Full Circle creates a Campaign Member record in Salesforce for every touchpoint this lead had
  • Multi-touch attribution model assigns credit: e.g. LinkedIn Ad (40%), Webinar (30%), Demo Form (30%)
  • Lead Source, Lead Source Detail, and all UTM fields are populated on the Lead record
  • Marketing now knows exactly which campaigns influenced this lead
⚡ Salesforce Milestone
Lead Created as MQL — Marketing Qualified Lead
Engagement criteria met · Campaign attribution complete · Ready for Sales handoff
5
Traction Complete — Lead Matching
Lead Matched to an Account
  • Traction Complete checks if this lead's org already exists as an Account in Salesforce
  • Matching uses: email domain, company name (fuzzy match), DUNS number
  • If a match is found, the Lead is linked to that Account — existing opportunities and history surface automatically
  • If no match, a net-new Account is prepared for creation
6
Traction Complete — Lead Routing
Lead Routed to the Right SDR
  • Routing rules fire: territory, org size, product line, SDR availability all evaluated
  • Lead is assigned to a specific SDR using round-robin or weighted queue
  • SLA timer starts — SDR must respond within a defined window (e.g. 1 hour)
  • SDR receives a Slack message and email: "New MQL assigned to you"
  • If SDR doesn't act in time, lead is auto-reassigned to the next available rep
7
Clay — Lead Enrichment
Lead Profile Enriched with Deep Research
  • Waterfall enrichment: Clay queries 150+ data providers sequentially (Apollo → Hunter → Lusha → Clearbit…) to find verified email and direct phone number
  • Firmographics: Annual revenue, employee count, current tech stack, CRM they're using today (Raiser's Edge? DonorPerfect?), recent funding
  • Claygent AI research: Reads the nonprofit's website, analyzes their 990 tax filings, profiles leadership team, finds recent news and milestones
  • Intent signals: Detects job changes, website visits, company mentions — flags if they're actively evaluating CRMs
  • All enriched data syncs back to the Salesforce Lead record — SDR now has a full dossier
8
SDR — Sales Development Rep
SDR Reviews Lead and Begins Outreach
  • SDR opens the enriched Lead record: sees campaign attribution history, Clay research, ICP grade, engagement score
  • Sends a personalized email referencing the org's specific situation (e.g. "I saw you're still on Raiser's Edge…")
  • Follows up with a LinkedIn message and phone call using the direct number from Clay
  • Runs a multi-touch sequence over 7–10 days if no response
  • Goal: qualify the lead (budget, authority, need, timeline) and get them on a call
9
Chili Piper — Meeting Booking
Demo Meeting Scheduled
  • Inbound path: If prospect filled out the demo form directly, Chili Piper's Form Concierge shows the right rep's calendar in under 5 seconds — prospect books instantly
  • Outbound path: SDR sends a personalized Chili Piper booking link — prospect picks a time slot, Distro routes to the correct AE
  • Calendar invite sent automatically with agenda and Zoom/Teams link
  • 24-hour and 1-hour reminder emails queued
  • Salesforce updated: Contact record, Opportunity record, and Calendar Event all created automatically
10
SDR / Account Executive
Discovery & Demo Call
  • SDR or AE joins the call with full context: org background, current CRM, pain points, campaign history
  • Explores the nonprofit's needs: donor database size, fundraising workflows, reporting requirements
  • Walks through a live demo of Neon One CRM tailored to their sector
  • Discusses migration path from their current tool (e.g. Blackbaud, DonorPerfect)
  • Pricing and timeline introduced
◆
Is this lead qualified?
ICP match  ·  Budget confirmed  ·  Decision-maker  ·  Clear timeline
✅ YES — Qualified
  • Lead converted to SQL (Sales Qualified Lead)
  • Salesforce: Lead → Account + Contact + Opportunity
  • Handed off to Account Executive (AE)
  • Full campaign attribution history follows the Opportunity
  • AE sends proposal and pricing deck
  • Deal progresses through pipeline to close
  • 🏆 Closed-Won → Neon One CRM customer
❌ NO — Not Qualified
  • Lead marked as disqualified with reason code
  • Added to a long-term nurture email sequence
  • Continues to receive Neon One content and campaigns
  • Full Circle keeps tracking future website visits and touchpoints
  • When intent signals spike again (e.g. they revisit /pricing), re-enters the pipeline at Stage 5
↩ Loops back to Stage 2 on next campaign touch
